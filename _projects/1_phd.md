My PhD explores **collaborative embodied learning of mathematics in virtual reality**. To organise the literature behind it, I built an interactive **Literature Universe**. It maps every paper I read against the five themes of the thesis: _Collaborative_, _Embodied_, _Learning_, _Mathematics_ and _Virtual Reality_.

#### What's inside

- **[Overview]({{ '/phd/index.html' | relative_url }}):** how often each theme, and each combination of themes, appears across the papers, shown as coverage bars, a heat map and a breakdown of combinations.
- **[Library]({{ '/phd/library.html' | relative_url }}):** a searchable gallery of every paper. Each paper has its own "DNA burst" fingerprint and a full analysis page.
- **[Constellation]({{ '/phd/constellation.html' | relative_url }}):** a 3D universe where planets are papers, lines link papers that share concepts, and golden stars mark research ideas that several papers already support.
- **[Analyze a paper]({{ '/phd/add-paper.html' | relative_url }}):** drop in a PDF and it is read entirely in the browser (nothing is uploaded). The page gives a summary, theme evidence, a concept lens and idea sparks.
