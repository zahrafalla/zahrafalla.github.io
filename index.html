Research

What I study

Serious Game Design

Games that teach. I design play that lets people explore ideas, practise decisions and learn together, from programming and computational thinking to mathematics.

Human–Computer Interaction

How people and interactive systems shape each other, and how to design for that.

Virtual Reality

Immersive environments as controlled, embodied laboratories for studying the mind.

Perceptual Decision-Making

How we turn noisy sensory evidence into choices, and how confident we feel about them.

Embodied Cognition

How the body, movement and sensorimotor feedback shape the way we think.

Educational Technology

Games and interactive tools that turn learning into something active and social.

Computational Cognitive Modeling

Drift diffusion models and Bayesian inference that explain behaviour, not just describe it.
