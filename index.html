01 About

Minds, bodies & virtual worlds

I am a Cognitive Science researcher (MSc, University of Tehran) working at the intersection of virtual reality, embodied interaction, and perceptual decision-making. I design Unity-based VR experiments and combine them with computational behavioral analysis, eye-tracking, and physiological measures to understand how people perceive, decide, and act in immersive environments.

My MSc thesis investigated how altered sensorimotor feedback in VR affects perceptual decision-making, confidence, cognitive load, sense of agency, and embodiment. I used Drift Diffusion Modeling (DDM) to quantify the cognitive costs of asynchronous visuomotor feedback. This work received the 2nd Place Poster Award at the Basic and Clinical Neuroscience Congress (BCN 2025), and a manuscript is in preparation for submission to HCI venues.

Beyond research, I mentor student projects in experimental psychology, have served as a Teaching Assistant for Computational Neuroscience at Neuromatch Academy, and founded Iran Minecraft Academy, where I design game-based learning environments for programming and computational thinking. I am also co-founder of Crange-Ghaf Game Studio, creating board and PC games that explore adaptive decision-making and collaboration.

I am broadly interested in collaborative learning, multimodal interaction, and cognitive technologies.
